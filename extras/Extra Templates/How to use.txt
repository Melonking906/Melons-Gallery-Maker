These are HTML templates you can use with your gallery!

To use a template:
1. Open Gallery maker click "Open the templates folder?" (Create it if none exists yet)
2. This will open a folder containing your gallery templates.
3. Pick one of these themes such as "Pixel Shop".
4. Copy all the files from the themes "templates" folder, to the templates folder you opened in step 1 (replacing any)
5. That's it, you can now regenerate your gallery and you should see the new template.

Remember you can modify template files however you like!
